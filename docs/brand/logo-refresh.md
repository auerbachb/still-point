# Issue #209 logo concept notes

These notes are written as text-only prompts for external image generation and review. No draft images are included in the repo.

## Concept A: Quiet orbit

Prompt: Create a calm app icon for "Still Point" on a warm near-black background. Use a centered glowing green point as the focus. Surround it with one thin, imperfect circular orbit line in warm off-white, with very subtle soft green light behind the center. Add four small rounded-square markers at the top, right, bottom, and left of the orbit to hint at meditation timer boxes without making the image feel like a dashboard. The mood should be quiet, premium, minimal, and meditative.

Why it works: This direction communicates the product promise in broad contexts: attention returning to a still center. The center point and orbit should stay legible at favicon and iOS sizes, while the small square markers connect back to the app's box-based timer.

Risks to watch: Avoid making the orbit look like a planet, atom, or generic wellness logo. The square markers should remain secondary.

## Concept B: Breathing boxes

Prompt: Create a minimal app icon for "Still Point" on a warm near-black background. Build the mark from a five-by-five grid of soft rounded squares. Most squares should be muted warm off-white with low opacity. The center square should glow green, and a few nearby squares should be faint green to suggest slow progress filling in over time. Keep the grid centered, spacious, and meditative rather than technical. Use subtle depth and glow, but no text.

Why it works: This direction directly references the core product interaction: time represented as boxes that fill during a meditation session. It is distinctive and tied to the app experience.

Risks to watch: At small sizes it may read as a productivity dashboard, keypad, or game board. The generated drafts should soften the grid and emphasize stillness.

## Concept C: Horizon point

Prompt: Create a serene app icon for "Still Point" with a warm dark background and a single green point sitting just above a soft horizontal horizon line. The horizon line should be thin, warm off-white, and slightly blurred at the edges. Add a faint circular breathing glow around the point. The composition should feel grounded, quiet, and spacious, like a place to return to, not a landscape illustration.

Why it works: This direction is simple, emotionally calm, and should scale well. It gives the brand a more atmospheric and less literal expression than the timer-box concepts.

Risks to watch: Avoid a sunrise, sunset, or nature-app look. The point must remain abstract and focused.

## Suggested first review choice

Start image generation with Concepts A and B. Concept A is the strongest likely final direction because it balances calm, focus, and small-size legibility while still nodding to the timer boxes. Concept B should be generated alongside it because it is the most product-specific alternate.

- Select one final direction and document the rationale.
- Export final assets for `ios/StillPointApp/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`, `src/app/favicon.ico`, Next web icon surfaces, and social metadata.
