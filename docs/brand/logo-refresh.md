# Still Point logo refresh

## Concepts reviewed

### Concept A: quiet orbit

- Mark: a centered point with a thin orbital ring and subtle cardinal blocks.
- Strengths: communicates calm, focus, and return; stays recognizable at favicon size; and uses the existing warm dark surface with green accent.
- Risks: less literal than the app's box-based meditation timer if used without supporting copy.

### Concept B: breathing boxes

- Mark: a five-by-five grid with calm green progress cells and a centered still point.
- Strengths: directly references the product's meditation timer boxes and feels distinctive without relying on text.
- Risks: reads more like a productivity dashboard at small sizes, where the grid cells compress into texture.

## Selected direction

Concept A is selected as the production direction. It conveys the product promise in the broadest contexts: attention returning to a still center. The orbital ring and center point remain legible in iOS, favicon, and Open Graph contexts, while the small blocks nod to the app's timer boxes without making the icon feel like a utility grid.
